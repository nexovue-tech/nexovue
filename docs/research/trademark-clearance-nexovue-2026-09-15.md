# Trademark clearance — NEXOVUE, 2026-09-15

> **Type:** Research snapshot. Not a living document — supersede with a new dated file rather than
> editing. Registry contents change daily; everything below is what the registers showed on
> 2026-09-15.
>
> **This is not a legal opinion and does not replace one.** It is the raw search evidence a
> trademark attorney would otherwise reproduce at cost. Hand them this file.
>
> **Purpose:** the brand is `nexovue`, the domain `nexovue.com` is held, and the name appears in 83
> places in the codebase. Before that becomes expensive to change, does anyone else already hold it?

## Answer

**Nobody owns NEXOVUE.** Zero records worldwide, zero in India, in any class.

**But a NEXOVUE application will probably draw an objection**, because two pending Indian marks sit
one letter away in the two classes a SaaS files in. Neither is registered yet. Neither stops you
using the name today — registration and use are different questions.

## Method, and what it does not cover

| | |
|---|---|
| **Searched** | IP India public search (`tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in`, logged in) and WIPO Global Brand Database (`branddb.wipo.int`, 76,689,088 records / 89 offices) |
| **Query** | Wordmark, **Contains**, `NEXO` — chosen over Start With because it also catches marks where the string is not at the front |
| **Classes** | **9** (downloadable software) and **42** (SaaS, cloud, software development) — the two a SaaS files in |
| **Live vs dead** | Abandoned, Refused, Removed and Withdrawn are treated as dead. They block nothing regardless of similarity |

**Not covered, and each could change the picture:**

1. **Phonetic search.** The Indian registry has a dedicated Phonetic mode, which is closer to how an
   examiner actually assesses similarity than a substring match. Not run.
2. **`NEXVUE` in the Indian registry.** WIPO showed no Indian NEXVUE, but that was not confirmed
   against IP India directly.
3. **Classes other than 9 and 42.** A mark in class 35 or 38 could still be cited.
4. **Common-law use.** An unregistered business trading under a similar name has rights in India
   through passing off, and appears in no register.
5. **WIPO lag.** WIPO mirrors national data and can trail it, so a very recent filing anywhere may
   not have appeared.

## Two arguments in nexovue's favour

**1. Every NEXOV\* mark is still pending.** All four are "Under Examination" or "Ready for
Examination" — none registered. They may be refused. Their earlier filing dates rank ahead of a
NEXOVUE application, so they would be cited, but they are not settled rights.

**2. The field is crowded, which narrows what each mark protects.** 59 live marks begin with `NEXO`
in class 9 alone — NEXOM, NEXON, NEXOR, NEXORA, NEXOLEX, NEXOFIX, NEXONA, nexoya, NEXOWA, NEXOTO,
NEXOXEN and more — coexisting on the same register. Where many similar marks already live side by
side, each gets narrower protection, because buyers are accustomed to distinguishing them. The
tables above are the evidence for that argument.

## What to ask an attorney

1. Do `NEXOVO` (7887958, cls 42) and `NEXOVA` (7212307 / 7938241, cls 9) get cited against a
   `NEXOVUE` application, and does the crowded NEXO field reduce their weight?
2. Does `NEXO` registered by Neebal Technologies (4107775, cls 42-adjacent tech, class 9) reach a
   longer mark that merely starts with those letters?
3. Run the phonetic search and the remaining classes — items 1 to 3 in *Not covered* above.
4. File as **ANANDHARAJ A** (individual rate, ₹4,500 per class; ₹9,000 for classes 9 and 42 together)
   now for the earlier priority date, and assign to the company on incorporation — the same written
   assignment that [ADR-004](../decisions/ADR-004-agpl-with-a-permissive-browser-sdk.md) and
   [ADR-005](../decisions/ADR-005-a-cla-rather-than-a-dco.md) already require for the copyright and
   the CLAs. One document should cover all three.
