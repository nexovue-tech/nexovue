# The F20 demo, as the founder gives it

**Say first: this is demo data, written by a seed, not a customer's traffic.** Run `demo.py --run`, then sign in as the
seeded owner and follow this, with the buyer table in `docs/features/dashboards/spec.md` open.

1. Land on the Findings feed (`/`). Show the finding the fault opened, and the three charts under it. Say nothing a rival
   does; say what nexovue shows.
2. Open **Dashboards**, start from **Service health** on the faulted service. Show the marks: the signed deploy as a rule,
   the finding as a shaded stretch. Say a release says when, never that it caused anything.
3. Build one: add a panel by keyboard only, move it, widen it, preview, save, reload.
4. Drag across a chart: the page says "Zoomed to ..." and Back returns. Copy a link and open it as another person.
5. Full screen on a viewer account: show "Leave full screen" on Tab, and the age of the reading.
6. Export, import into a new dashboard, and try a hostile file: nothing opens.
7. Ask the buyer-table questions (query language, public links, dashboards as code): the answers are "no", said plainly.
